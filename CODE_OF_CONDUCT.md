# Code of conduct

We want a respectful, practical community where people can contribute regardless
of background, identity or experience.

Be considerate, assume good intent, accept constructive criticism and focus
feedback on the work. Respect privacy and boundaries. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing and repeated personal attacks are unacceptable.
These expectations apply to repository discussions and related community spaces.

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer through an available private
contact channel. Do not post private details in public issues. Before a public
community launch, maintainers must publish a dedicated reporting contact.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, limit disclosure to those handling the
report, and may remove content, warn participants or restrict participation.
Serious or repeated violations may result in a permanent ban. Maintainers are
subject to the same standards.
